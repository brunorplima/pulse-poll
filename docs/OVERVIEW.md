# PulsePoll

**Capturing the Pulse of Decision-Making Through Simple Polling**

In today's fast-paced world, making decisions collectively shouldn't be a hurdle. PulsePoll is a streamlined web application that epitomizes simplicity and efficiency in gathering opinions. Whether you're deciding on a movie night pick, gauging opinions on topical issues, or settling debates, PulsePoll is your go-to platform for creating, sharing, and viewing poll results.

---

## Key Features

### Seamless Poll Creation
Users can craft polls with ease, inputting their questions and specifying up to 10 multiple-choice answers. PulsePoll's intuitive interface ensures that setting up your poll is a hassle-free experience, encouraging broader participation. Choose whether your poll is public (visible to everyone) or private (accessible only via direct link).

### Instant Sharing
Once a poll is live, PulsePoll generates a unique link for easy distribution. This feature amplifies reach, allowing anyone with the link to contribute their vote without needing an account.

### Results at a Glance
Poll creators and participants can view vote tallies for each option, providing insight into collective preferences.

### Public Poll Discovery
Browse public polls created by the community. Explore what others are asking and contribute your vote to ongoing discussions.

### User Dashboard
Registered users gain access to a simple dashboard that lists their created polls and allows them to view results.

---

## Why PulsePoll?

PulsePoll distinguishes itself through its user-centric design, emphasizing ease of use. It caters to casual users looking to make quick decisions and anyone seeking to gather feedback informally.

Whether for personal use, event planning, education, or informal research, PulsePoll delivers a simple platform that puts the power of polling at your fingertips.

## Post-MVP Roadmap 🔮

Future enhancements planned for PulsePoll include:

- **Real-time results** - Watch votes tally live as they come in
- **Visual charts and graphs** - Enhanced data visualization
- **Poll expiration** - Set automatic closing dates for polls
- **Vote tracking** - Prevent duplicate voting via IP/cookies
- **Social sharing** - Direct sharing to social networks and email
- **Advanced discovery** - Search, filters, and sorting for poll browsing
- **Poll management** - Delete polls and view detailed analytics
